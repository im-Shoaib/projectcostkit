How we handle your insurance claim

Most storm roofs are paid for by insurance. You pay your deductible, and we do the legwork in between.

  1. Inspect

    We walk the roof, test-square the slopes and check gutters, vents and siding for hail hits.

  2. Document

    You get a photo report with every damaged area marked, ready to send to your insurer.

  3. Meet the adjuster

    We are on the roof with your adjuster so nothing gets missed or written off.

  4. Rebuild

    Once the claim is approved we schedule your install, usually within two weeks.

Questions we hear every week

Will my insurance pay for a new roof?

If the damage came from a covered event like hail or wind, usually yes, minus your deductible. We inspect first and tell you honestly if we do not see enough damage for a claim.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most asphalt shingle roofs are done in one day. Metal, tile and cedar take longer, and we give you the schedule in writing before we start.

Do I need to be home during the install?

No. We just need access to the driveway and an outdoor power outlet. Your project manager sends photos when the crew finishes.

What if it rains during the job?

We only tear off what we can dry in the same day, and we watch the forecast closely. Your home is never left open overnight.
