What we paint

Every job gets the same prep standard, a written scope and a crew lead who is on site from the first day to the last.

Exterior painting

Siding, trim, doors and shutters, with full scraping and spot priming before a drop of topcoat goes on.

  • Power wash and scrape
  • Caulk gaps and seams
  • Two finish coats

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings and trim with furniture moved, floors covered and the room put back the way you left it.

  • Patch and sand walls
  • Low-odor paints
  • Daily clean-up

Cabinet refinishing

A factory-smooth sprayed finish on your existing cabinets for a fraction of what new ones cost.

  • Degrease and sand
  • Bonding primer
  • Sprayed enamel finish

Deck and fence staining

Cleaning, brightening and staining that brings tired gray wood back and protects it for seasons.

  • Wash and brighten
  • Sand rough boards
  • Penetrating stain

How a job runs

No surprises halfway through. You know the price, the dates and who is in charge before we start.

  1. Walkthrough

    We visit, measure and look closely at the surfaces, usually within three days of your call.

  2. Written quote

    A fixed price with the prep, products and number of coats spelled out line by line.

  3. Prep and paint

    Your crew lead sends a short update at the end of every day, with photos.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We go over every wall with you, touch up anything you point at, then clean up.
