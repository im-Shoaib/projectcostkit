Questions contractors ask

Why is it this affordable?

We hand-code lightweight sites instead of paying for page builders, plugins and agency overhead, and we reuse proven layouts like the demos above. You pay for the work on your site, not for an office.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. The plan price is paid once. The only ongoing cost is your domain renewal, paid to your registrar. Hosting for sites like these can be free.

Can you make it look different from the demos?

Yes. The demos show the quality and the features. Your site gets your own colors, fonts, photos and wording, and we can mix sections from different demos.

I do not have good photos. Is that a problem?

Phone photos of real jobs work well, especially before and after shots. Until you have enough, we can use clean illustrations like the ones on the demos.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes. We build for home service businesses in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Europe. Everything is handled by WhatsApp and email, in English.

What happens after I message you?

We reply with a few questions about your business and a fixed price for the plan that fits. Nothing starts until you agree to it.
